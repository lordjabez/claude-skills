## Interview opener

```text
I'm Jud, part of the leadership team, though I joined recently myself, and glad
to be talking with you. Most of this hour is about your experiences, and I'd
like real examples for each question. I'll be typing as we go, so if I glance
away it's not boredom, just trying to keep up. No secret grocery lists, I swear!

*Follow-ups: Listen for specific actions. Ask who else was involved. What got in
the way? How did it end up? What would you change?*

Tell me about a time you saw a plan slipping before anyone else did. What did
you do about it? How did it turn out?
```


## Training design

```text
Participants
- Crew (project team)
- Stand-ins - play the part of a real client
- Facilitator (cannot be on either Crew or Client teams)
- Sponsor (may double as facilitator)

Three Phases
1. Kickoff
   a. Crew review prework document.
   b. Crew draft agenda and questions for the kickoff session.
   c. Facilitator reviews agenda (async is fine) and okays the session.
   d. Crew schedule and lead the kickoff session.
2. Planning
   a. Crew write up the plan and timeline in a one-page brief.
   b. Stand-ins sign off (or send back to Crew for another pass).

Prework Template
- Project Title: (one liner)
- Expected Outcomes: (list deliverables here, but focus on what changes for the user)
```

## 1-on-1 agenda and first-meeting questions

```text
Agenda
- Highlight / lowlight since we last talked
- How can I help you this upcoming week?
- What's the status of your current annual goals?
- Open discussion (you pick the topic)

First 1-on-1 (how you prefer to be managed)
- What do you most need from me as a manager?
- How do you prefer to receive feedback? In writing? In person? Another way?
- How can I know when you're struggling and need help?
- Are there any manager behaviors that particularly bother you?

Feedback on me
- How can I help you be more successful?
- How am I doing? What can I be doing better?
```
