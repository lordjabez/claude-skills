# Palette

Transcribed from the "Colors of the Faith" sheet of `Style of the Faith.xlsx`.
Each column of the sheet is a family; steps run lightest (1) to darkest (4).
Neutrals continue to step 7.

Contrast is the WCAG ratio against Parchment Crease (`#F4EFE6`), the default
page. 4.5 or higher is safe for body text, 3.0 or higher for large text and
graphical marks only.

Step 1 of each family is a tint for backgrounds, callouts, and table banding.
Steps 2 and 3 are for fills and chart marks. Step 4 is for text and strong
marks.

## Text-safe on paper (4.5:1 or better)

Library Oak 7.0, Speakeasy Crimson 8.3, Well-Watered Plant 5.0, Patinaed
Filament 5.2, College Ivy Vine 6.5, Vestment Navy 6.8, Longest Night 10.8,
Dusk Violet 6.2, Shadow Plum 8.2, plus neutrals 5 to 7.

Large text only (3:1): Absinthe Coupe 4.1, Radiator Rust 4.0, Hearthstone 3.6,
Threshold Cobblestone 3.4, Camel Cashmere Coat 3.3.

Illuminated Brass (2.6) and Filament Amber (1.4) are not text colors on paper.

## Text-safe on Longest Night

## Chart series order

For categorical data on a paper ground, assign in this order and stop adding
hues before you run out of meaning. Past five series, group or facet instead.

1. Vestment Navy `#335384`
2. Filament Amber `#FEC500`
3. Hearthstone `#BE645A`
4. Rosemary Garnish `#67A776`
5. Dusk Violet `#724B75`
6. Camel Cashmere Coat `#A67C52`

For sequential data, run one family from step 1 to step 4. For diverging data,
pair Blue and Orange through a Parchment Crease midpoint.
